## Friendlier quantities when changing servings

Goal: a scaled recipe reads like something you'd measure: "⅓ cup" or "2 tbsp", never "0.42 cup". Servings keep stepping by one; the scaled amounts are rounded to real kitchen measures instead. This applies wherever amounts are scaled: the recipe screen's stepper, the cook screen, and Add to list (servings card and multiplier pills), including what's actually added to the list. The recipe's own amounts at its base servings are shown as written, never rounded. Spec: add the rounding rules to 4.5 (servings).

- [x] Logic: `kitchenRound(amount)` in `src/domain/` (next to `quantity-math.ts`), used by `scaledAmount` in `src/features/recipes/recipe-view.ts` and by `buildRecipeAdds` in `src/features/recipes/add-to-list.ts` when the multiplier isn't 1. **Rules:** cups round to the nearest of ¼, ⅓, ½, ⅔, ¾ steps; under ¼ cup becomes tablespoons (16 to a cup). Tablespoons round to the nearest ½; under 1 tbsp becomes teaspoons (3 to a tbsp), and 3 tbsp or more becomes cups only when that lands on a clean cup fraction. Teaspoons round to the nearest ¼, with ⅛ the smallest. Grams round to 5 (to 10 from 100 up), ounces and pounds to the nearest ¼, ml to 5. A count with no unit (onions, eggs) rounds to the nearest ½, never below ½; units the app doesn't know round to 2 significant figures. Nothing scales to 0. Use the unit keys the parser already has; read `src/domain/` before adding any. Then format as now (`formatAmount`). Unit tests for each rule, the conversions, the "only when scaled" rule, and a whole recipe scaled 4 → 6 and 4 → 1 reading sensibly.
