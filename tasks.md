## Push notifications

Goal: household members hear about each other's list activity when the app is closed: "Alex added milk, eggs and 4 more to Groceries" and "Alex is shopping at Groceries". This is spec 11.1 coming out of Later, and the in-app banners (4.11) are the base it builds on. iOS only (Android isn't a target, but nothing here should rule it out: keep the Android channel call and the `platform` column). Delivery is Expo Push (`expo-notifications`, which talks to APNs for us). Spec 11.1 says to queue from a trigger on `list_items` inserts, but a Combine adds a **contribution** to an existing open item and inserts no `list_items` row. So "added" events come from `list_item_contributions` inserts, and "shopping" events from `list_items.checked_at` going from null to set. A trigger queues each event on a pgmq queue `household_events`, as stash does for captures. A worker Edge Function `send-notifications`, run by pg_cron every minute, groups the events and sends one push per group to the household's other members. The person who made the change never gets a push about it.

**Grouping.** A group is (household, actor, list, kind). It sends once it has been quiet for 60 s (its newest event is at least 60 s old) or its oldest event is 5 minutes old, whichever comes first, so a burst of adds becomes one push. At send time the worker re-reads the rows, so an add that was undone (the contribution is soft-deleted) or an item unchecked again doesn't count, and a group left with nothing is dropped without sending. **Added**: counts the live contributions. The body names up to two items by `display_name`, then "and N more". A group that is all one recipe's add reads "Alex added Tacos (8 items) to Groceries". **Shopping**: sent at most once per (actor, list) in a 2-hour window, on the first checks after none for 2 hours, never once per check. **Stale events**: a device that was offline in the store syncs its checks hours later. Drop events whose row time (the contribution's `created_at`, the item's `checked_at`) is more than 15 minutes older than the worker's clock, so nobody hears "Alex is shopping" after Alex got home. **Actor name**: the first word of `profiles.display_name`.

**Recipients.** A recipient is any current member of the household, other than the actor, who has at least one push token, has that kind turned on, and isn't in their quiet hours, judged in their own time zone. Membership is read when the push is sent, so someone who left the household gets nothing. Tapping a push opens that list (`/list/<id>`).

## Asking about an item

Goal: in the store, you open an item you don't recognise and tap **Ask Alex for a photo** (or **Ask Alex a question**). Alex gets a push, answers with a photo, a note or both, and the answer shows on the item for everyone in the household. Mockups: `docs/design/item-requests/` boards C–G (`c-ask-for-photo`, `d-request-sent`, `e-push`, `f-answer`, `g-photo-arrives`), canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/YDjSWr3wp7Wtf3PZad79j2. This builds on the Push notifications section above (`push_tokens`, `notification_settings`, the `household_events` queue, the `send-notifications` worker, tap routing) and on "Who added it" (the "Added by" row and avatars). Don't start it before both are done.

**Who you ask.** The person who added the item: the member behind its earliest live contribution with an `added_by` that isn't you (a recipe add counts as whoever added the recipe). With several other adders, the button reads **Ask for a photo** and opens a native menu of their short names. No other adder (only you, Siri on your account, or rows with no `added_by`), or a household with only you in it: the card doesn't show. Nor does it on a checked item, or offline (it needs a connection, like Leave household; the card shows the usual offline note instead of its buttons).

**A request.** One row per ask, `kind` `photo` or `question` (a question carries up to 200 chars of text). While you have an open request on an item, its row shows **Photo requested · Waiting** (board D) and tapping it again does nothing; the toast's **Undo** cancels it (and its push, if not sent yet). The person asked can answer with a photo, a note (up to 500 chars), or both, or tap **Can't help right now** (declined: the asker's row reads "Alex can't help right now" and the card offers asking again). An answer shows on the item sheet for every member (board G): the photo full width at 4:3 in a `Card`, the note in quotes under it with the answerer's avatar, name and time, and a plain `primary` **Ask Alex again**. Several answers stack, newest first. Requests and answers belong to the item's current round: when a checked item is reopened by a new add (4.4: its contributions are replaced), requests made before the reopen stop showing (`created_at` before its oldest live contribution) and are cleaned up with the item later.

**Photos** go in a private bucket `item-photos` at `<household_id>/<request_id>/<uuid>.jpg` (jpeg, 2 MB), shrunk on the device like recipe photos (`downscalePhoto`, `UPLOAD_PHOTO`), with the same member-only policies and the same server-side cleanup through `photo-cleanup` as `recipe-photos` (`supabase/migrations/20261029120000_recipe_photo_storage.sql`): an object goes when its request goes, its item is purged, or its household is deleted.

**Pushes.** To the person asked: title the list's name, body "Sam wants a photo of *za'atar*" (or "Sam asked about *za'atar*: Which brand?"), with iOS actions **Take photo** (opens the app on the answer screen with the camera up) and **Reply** (a text-input action that sends the note without opening the app). To the asker, when answered: "Alex sent a photo of *za'atar*" / "Alex replied about *za'atar*" / "Alex can't help right now", opening the item sheet. These aren't grouped like adds: they go out right away (the asker is in the store). Both follow the recipient's quiet hours and a new **Requests from your household** switch.

**By hand, not by Ralph** (the user runs anything hosted, see memory): `supabase db push`, `supabase functions deploy send-notifications`, and a new development build after the notification categories change. Hand these over in the progress.md entry. Push needs a real device, so every push check is a manual check.

- [ ] Server: `item_requests` and the `item-photos` bucket. New migration. **`public.item_requests`**: `id uuid primary key`, `household_id uuid not null references households on delete cascade`, `list_item_id uuid not null references list_items on delete cascade`, `requested_by uuid references auth.users on delete set null`, `requested_of uuid references auth.users on delete set null`, `kind text not null check (kind in ('photo','question'))`, `question text` (1–200 chars, only with `kind = 'question'`), `status text not null default 'open' check (status in ('open','answered','declined','cancelled'))`, `answer_note text` (1–500 chars), `answer_photo_path text` (a path under its household and request in `item-photos`), `answered_at timestamptz`, `created_at`, `updated_at`, with an index on `list_item_id` and a partial unique index on `(list_item_id, requested_by) where status = 'open'`. RLS: `select` for household members only; no direct writes. Writes go through `security definer` RPCs granted to `authenticated`: `request_item_info(p_id uuid, p_list_item_id uuid, p_requested_of uuid, p_kind text, p_question text)` (the caller and `requested_of` are both current members of the item's household and differ, the item is unchecked and not deleted; an existing open request by the caller on the item is returned as is, so a retry is safe), `cancel_item_request(p_id)` (the asker, while open), `answer_item_request(p_id, p_note text, p_photo_path text)` (the person asked, while open or answered, so a second photo can follow; at least one of note or photo; sets `answered`, `answered_at`) and `decline_item_request(p_id)` (the person asked, while open). Each raises a named error for each refusal. **Bucket** `item-photos` as `recipe-photos` (private, jpeg, 2 MB; member select/insert/delete on the first folder; no update), and extend the photo-cleanup queue: an `answer_photo_path` replaced, a request row deleted, a household deleted. Add a daily pg_cron job that deletes requests whose item is checked or deleted and that are older than 30 days. pgTAP in a new `supabase/tests/` file: each RPC's happy path and each refusal (not a member, asking yourself, a former member, a checked item, a second open request returns the first, answering someone else's request, cancelling after an answer), RLS (another household can't read), the bucket policies, cleanup queueing, and that `admin.delete_user` and household deletion still pass.
