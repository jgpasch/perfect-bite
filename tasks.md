## Recipe source credit

Goal: an imported recipe always says where it came from, next to the site's own steps and photo.

- [x] Credit the recipe's source (spec 4.5, 4.10). Check first what the recipe screen shows today (`src/app/recipe/[id]/index.tsx`: the header's link row) and note it in progress.md. A recipe whose `source_url` is a link that opens (`recipeLinkUrl`) shows a credit line "From <host>" (host without `www.`, e.g. "From seriouseats.com") at the foot of the Method card, under the steps, and at the foot of Cook mode's last step. Tapping it opens the original in Safari, as the link row does. VoiceOver: "Recipe from <host>, link". When the recipe has its photo from the site, the same credit sits on the photo too (a small caption, `textSecondary`, per DESIGN.md). It follows the link: editing the link changes the credit, and removing the link removes it (the recipe is then the user's own). A recipe without steps or a link shows none. Tests: credit shown with a link and steps, its host and press, gone when the link is removed, none for a recipe typed by hand. Update spec 4.5.
