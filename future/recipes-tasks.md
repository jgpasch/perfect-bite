# Recipes: later

<!-- Status: ideas, not tasks yet. Spec: 4.5, 4.10, 6.6. -->

- **Save and show the method steps.** Today a recipe is a shopping template: name, notes, source URL, tags, time and ingredients (`src/db/schema.ts`), so cooking means going back to the website, often paywalled or full of ads. The page's JSON-LD already has `recipeInstructions` (see the fixtures in `src/shared/__tests__/fixtures/recipe-jsonld/`), but `findRecipe` (`src/shared/recipe-jsonld.ts`) and `ExtractedRecipe` (`src/shared/schemas/recipe-import.ts`) drop it. New parts: a `steps` column (ordered text, synced like the rest of the recipe), reading `recipeInstructions` (strings, `HowToStep`, `HowToSection`) in the JSON-LD reader, steps in the LLM extraction output (a prompt bump to v3, which invalidates the `recipe_imports` cache; mind the "byte-for-byte v2" test from the AI tags work), a Method card under Ingredients on the recipe screen, and editing steps for hand-made recipes. A reused recipe (same canonical URL) keeps its own steps.
- **Cooking mode** (after the steps). A full-screen view from the recipe screen: the screen stays awake (`expo-keep-awake`, not installed yet), large Dynamic Type text, ingredients and steps you can tick off as you go (local only, not synced, cleared when you leave), and swipe or tap between steps.
- **Real servings and scaling.** Import reads a number of servings (`readServings` in `recipe-jsonld.ts`, `servings` in `ExtractedRecipe`) but only keeps it as the note "Serves 4" (`src/features/recipes/import.ts:275`), so Add to list's ½×/1×/2×/3× (`src/app/recipe/[id]/add-to-list.tsx`) scales from an unknown base and "I'm cooking for 6" can't be done. New parts: a nullable `servings` column filled on import (no more "Serves N" note), editable on the recipe screen; a servings stepper there that scales the amounts shown (with `quantity-math`, display only); and Add to list offering servings ("for 6") when the recipe knows its base, falling back to the multiplier when it doesn't.
- **Recipe photo from the page.** Recipe cards are an icon tile and text, which makes a long list hard to scan. Read the JSON-LD `image` (string, array, or `ImageObject`) and an `og:image` fallback, store only the URL in a nullable `image_url` column, and load it remotely with `expo-image` (already installed) and its disk cache, with the icon tile as the placeholder and when loading fails. Show it on the Recipes tab cards and as a header on the recipe screen. No upload or storage of our own; a hand-made recipe has no photo (adding one from the camera roll is a separate, later idea).
