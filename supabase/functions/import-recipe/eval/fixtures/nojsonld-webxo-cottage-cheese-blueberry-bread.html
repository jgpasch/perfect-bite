High-Protein Cottage Cheese Blueberry Bread

By Webxo · May 2, 2026

This loaf tastes like a blueberry muffin but packs about 12 grams of protein a slice, thanks to cottage cheese and eggs. Blend the wet ingredients until smooth so the cottage cheese disappears into the crumb.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups oat flour
  • 1/4 cup maple syrup
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 cup blueberries

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a 9x5-inch loaf pan with parchment.
  2. Blend the cottage cheese, eggs, maple syrup and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Stir in the oat flour, baking powder and salt until just combined.
  4. Fold in the blueberries, keeping a few to scatter on top.
  5. Bake for 45 to 50 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes before slicing.

Keeps for 4 days in the fridge, or freeze the slices and toast them from frozen.

  1. Made this with frozen blueberries, worked great. I used 2 tbsp honey instead.
