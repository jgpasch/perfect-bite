Weeknight Chickpea Curry

Ingredients

  • 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • Salt & pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Rinse the rice.
  2. Simmer everything for 20–25 minutes.
