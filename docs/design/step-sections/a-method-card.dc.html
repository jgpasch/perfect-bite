Chicken Thigh Marinade
familystylefood.com · 45 min
Method
7 steps
Marinate the chicken
1
Whisk the marinade ingredients together in a bowl.
2
Pour it over the chicken and turn the thighs to coat.
3
Marinate 20–30 minutes at room temperature, or 12–24 hours in the fridge.
Then cook it one way
4 ways
4
Preheat the grill to medium-high (400–425°F).
5
Lift the chicken out of the marinade and season with salt and pepper.
6
Grill skin-side up 5 minutes, then flip onto indirect heat.
7
Cover and cook 5–8 minutes more, to 165°F. Rest 5 minutes.
