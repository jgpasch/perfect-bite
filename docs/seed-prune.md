## How to use it

- A ticked name (`- [x]`) is removed from the seed; an unticked one (`- [ ]`) stays.
- Tick or untick anything, in either list. Don't move names between lists or categories: only the tick counts.
- When you are done, change `Reviewed: no` above to `Reviewed: yes`. Nothing is removed until then (the "Apply the prune list" task in `future/i18n-es-mx-tasks.md` checks that line).

What is suggested for removal:

- **Brands** (`cheerios`, `tide pods`, `hellmann's mayo`), including ones many people type as the everyday name (`velveeta`, `cool whip`, `rotel`, `nutella`, `old bay`, `crisco`). Untick any you want to keep. Liqueurs that recipes call for by name (`kahlua`, `cointreau`, `aperol`) are kept.
- **Prepared foods, platters and gifts** (`fruit tray`, `chocolate covered strawberries`, `candy apple`, `deli sandwich`, `box of chocolates`, `gift wrap`).
- **Near-duplicates of a name that stays**: a pack size, count or container added to the name (`ears of corn` beside `corn`, `bag of onions`, `cilantro bunch`, `gallon of milk`, `dozen eggs`, `chili seasoning packet`), `organic` versions, and reworded twins (`orzo pasta` beside `orzo`, `sriracha sauce` beside `sriracha`). Spelling variants people type either way (`fettuccine`/`fettuccini`, `bbq sauce`/`barbecue sauce`) are kept.
- **Things rarely on a US grocery list**: produce few stores carry (`casaba melon`, `ugli fruit`), non-grocery goods (`antifreeze`, `power strip`, `dog leash`, `fish tank`), and vague names (`sauce`, `paper`).

When in doubt, a name is kept.
