# Design: direction B, "Pantry"

Friendly and tactile: soft white cards sit on a pale green ground, checks are round, and titles are set in a chunky grotesque. Every aisle has its own colour, so you can find your place in a long list at a glance.

The reference boards are in `docs/design/pantry/` (`lists.html` 01–04, `recipes.html` 05–08, where 08's "already on the list" step is retired: recipe adds Combine like single adds, spec 4.4, `settings.html` 09–12; board 01, the Lists home, is retired). Treat their 390×844 phone frames as the reference. The List tab's switcher, the Your lists sheet and its invite rows follow boards E, G and H of the mockup canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/M587vDvPQqF4KYAZEigusC. The boards' floating tab bar is a mock: the app keeps the native tab bar (`NativeTabs`) and native headers, tinted `primary`.

## Rules

- Every colour, size, radius and font comes from `useTheme()` (`src/hooks/use-theme.ts`), which reads `src/constants/theme.ts`. No hex values or font sizes in components.
- Keep Dynamic Type on: never set `allowFontScaling={false}` or fix a text container's height.
- Touch targets are at least 44 pt (`sizes.minTapTarget`). Make a smaller glyph's hit area bigger rather than the glyph.
- Text contrast is at least 4.5:1 in both schemes. `src/constants/__tests__/theme.test.ts` checks the pairs below; add a pair there when you put text on a new ground.
- Light and dark follow the system setting. Shadows are light mode only.

## Aisle colours (`aisles.*`)

Each hue has a `dot` (section dots, the aisle-order dots, list colour dots), a `soft` tint (icon wells, a just-added row flash in that aisle, household header bands) and a `strong` shade for fills that carry `onPrimary` text (the selected aisle pill). In light mode most dots are under 4.5:1 with white text (blue 4.4, rose 4.4, grey 3.7, amber 2.9), so `strong` is a deeper shade there (green `#2E6B45`, teal `#1F6E68`, blue `#2F6396`, plum `#6E4594`, rose `#A33D53`, orange `#A04A1C`, amber `#8A5C0C`, grey `#55635A`); in dark mode it equals the dot, with dark `onPrimary` text.

| Hue | Light dot / soft | Aisles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| green | `#2E6B45` / `#DDEFE2` | produce, pet |
| teal | `#2A8C84` / `#DCEFEC` | frozen, household |
| blue | `#3E7BB8` / `#DFEAF6` | dairy-eggs, personal-care, baby |
| plum | `#8A5BB0` / `#ECE3F5` | snacks, beverages |
| rose | `#C2556B` / `#F7E1E6` | meat-seafood, deli |
| orange | `#D0703A` / `#FAE4D6` | canned-jarred, spices, condiments |
| amber | `#C98A1E` / `#F6EBD4` | bakery, pantry, pasta-grains, baking, breakfast |
| grey | `#7C877F` / `#EEF3EC` | other, and any slug the app doesn't know (custom aisles) |

Item rows use `categoryColors(slug, scheme, style)` (`appearance.ts`), which returns `{ ring, checkFill, tint, text, pill? }` for the Item rows setting (`ItemRowStyle`): the `dot` as the check's ring, `surface` inside the check, and the `strong` shade for the amount. **Colour bands** (`bands`, the default): the aisle's `soft` is the row's ground (`tint`), amount falling back to `inkSecondary` where `strong` is under 4.5:1. **White tiles** (`tiles`): `surface` is the row's ground and the amount sits on a `soft` pill (`pill`), falling back to `ink`. Sorting… and unknown slugs get grey. Use it for any new per-item category UI.

The rule: pick the hue with `aisleColor(slug)` from `src/domain/aisle-color.ts`, then read `aisles[hue].dot` or `.soft`. Never colour an aisle by its position, so an aisle keeps its colour when the store order changes. Lists take only green, blue, rose, amber and grey (`LIST_HUES`, the `lists.color` check); teal, plum and orange are aisle-only. A dot is decoration: the aisle's name is always shown next to it, so colour is never the only signal.

## Spacing, radius and sizes

- `spacing`: 4 pt base (`xxs` 2 … `xxxl` 48). Cards sit `lg` (16) in from the screen edge; titles sit 20 in (`lg` + `xs`).
- `rounded`: `card` 22, `sheet` 28 (top corners), `row` 16 (aisle-order rows), `md` 12 (segmented track, icon wells), `full` for pills, round checks, round buttons and CTAs.
- `sizes.minTapTarget` 44. Rows inside cards are at least 50 pt tall (`sizes.cardRowMin`).
- Component sizes: `check` 24 (`checkBorder` 2), `dot` 10, `pill` 40, `segment` 34 (`segmentInset` 3), `viewButton` 32 (the list header's view pill, 44 pt hit area), `cta` 54, `softButton` 48, `bar` 52 (add bar input, toast), `barButton` 40 (the + and Undo), `focusRing` 2, `iconWell` 36 (recipe icon tiles in card headers), `sheetIcon` 52 (the item sheet's icon tile), `recipeTile` 56 (a recipe's icon tile on its card in the Recipes tab), `removeButton` 32 (round − on "Added" rows), `fieldLabel` 76 (the label column of Name / Amount / Note rows, a minimum), `settingsIcon` 32 (the icon tile at the start of a settings row), `accountAvatar` 52, `avatar` 48 with a 3 pt `avatarRing` (household members), `signInShape` 92, `aisleRow` 52 (a minimum) and `grip` 20 (Aisle order rows). Radii `badge` 10 and `segment` 11.
- `shadows.tile`: an item tile on the list screen (White tiles), 1 pt down, 3 pt blur, 10%, `ink`.
- `shadows.lifted`: the row being dragged on the Aisle order screen (14 pt down, 30 pt blur, 22%, `ink`).

## Components (`src/components/`)

`Card`, `CheckCircle`, `SectionHeader`, `IconTile` (a rounded well in an aisle hue's `soft` with the glyph in its `dot`; recipes pick the hue with `recipeHue(id)`), `Pill`, `SegmentedControl`, `PrimaryButton` / `SoftDangerButton` / `SurfaceButton` (`button.tsx`; `Button` is left for secondary and plain actions, with an optional text `color`), `SettingsSection` / `SettingsRow` (`settings-group.tsx`), `Badge`, `AddBar` and `Toast`. Which tokens each uses in each state lives in `appearance.ts`, which is tested for contrast in both schemes. Controls under 44 pt (pills, the + and Undo buttons, segments) get `hitSlop` up to 44.

## Component patterns

- **Card**: `surface`, radius `card`, 16 pt side margin, `overflow: hidden`. Rows inside are separated by a 1 pt `border` hairline (none above the first row). Cards stack with a 10–12 pt gap.
- **Row with round check**: check, then the name (`body`, flexible), then the amount (`amount`). 12 pt gap, 16 pt side padding, min height 50. A second line (`footnote`, `inkSecondary`) goes under the name. On the list screen, unchecked rows carry their aisle colour (`categoryColors`), in one of two styles chosen on Settings > Aisle colours > Item rows (per device): **Colour bands** (default) fill each row with its aisle's `soft`, 2 pt apart in the card's `surface`; **White tiles** make each row a `surface` tile (radius `row`, `shadows.tile` in light mode, corners clipping the swipe panels) 4 pt apart, and the card holding them takes `background` instead of `surface`, its header too (`FoldingSection ground`). Either way the check is ringed in the aisle `dot` and filled `surface`, the name is `itemName`, and there's no aisle dot by the name; tiles put the amount on a `soft` pill. With By aisle on Header only, rows stay plain whichever style is set. Checked rows are untinted: filled check, name struck through in `inkSecondary` (the boards use `inkTertiary`, but that's 3.7:1, under the 4.5:1 text rule), the amount in `inkSecondary`, hairlines between. The row an add just merged into takes its aisle's `soft` tint while the toast shows.
- **Suggestion chips** (list screen, `SuggestionChips`): one horizontal row of 40 pt `surface` chips (radius `full`, `amount` text, 8 pt apart) docked 8 pt above the add bar, scrolling edge to edge. The typed part is `primary` 700. Recipe chips lead, with a `primary` `fork.knife` glyph and a 1 pt `primary` outline (`sizes.chipBorder`). Autocomplete only while the field has text; an empty field has no row, and no chips means no row. The row grows the add bar upward, so the field itself never moves. The add field has autocorrect, spell check and autofill off, so iOS's QuickType bar never shows; the chips are the only suggestions.
- **List header** (list screen, the List tab's root): the native header and the title band below it are `header` green with light status bar content; the name in `screenTitle` `onHeader` is the list switcher (below), "7 to get" in `onHeaderSecondary`, and under it, aligned with the title, the view button in `listHeaderColors().viewButton`: a 32 pt fully rounded pill in a 14% white wash with the current view ("As added") in `amount` `onHeader` and a small `chevron.down`, padded to a 44 pt hit area. It opens a native menu (`@expo/ui` `Menu`) of the three views, each with an SF Symbol (`clock`, `cart`, `fork.knife`), the current one ticked. Header buttons are tinted `onHeader`. The list's ground sits on it as a sheet with `card`-radius top corners overlapping the green.
- **List add mode**: while the add field is focused, the title and view button collapse (250 ms shrink, lift and fade; fade only under Reduce Motion) and the native header's title becomes the list's name in `headline` `onHeader` over "8 to get · As added" in `footnote` `onHeaderSecondary`. Cards keep their 16 pt inset and `card` radius, so only the title moves. A row matching the typed text takes `primarySoft`, with the typed part and "already on list" (in place of the amount, `amount`) in `onPrimarySoft`.
- **Round check**: 24 pt circle, 2 pt `controlBorder` ring when off (or a given `ring`, e.g. the item's aisle colour); filled `primary` with an `onPrimary` tick when on. "Sorting…" items show a dashed ring (`aisles.amber.dot`, or grey on the tinted list rows) and a `pending` "Sorting…" label; on the list screen their rows take the neutral grey tint. The whole row is the tap target.
- **Section header**: aisle `dot` (10 pt circle), the name in `sectionTitle`, then the count in `inkSecondary` (13 / 600) on the right. Padding 14 top, 6 bottom, 16 sides. Recipe sections use a 36 pt rounded icon well in place of the dot. With Settings > Aisle colours set to Header only, By aisle drops the dot and makes the header a band (`aisleHeaderBand`): the aisle's `soft` ground with the name, count and chevron in its `strong` (or `ink` under 4.5:1), padded 12 top and bottom so a folded card is just the band; the rows under it are untinted, with hairlines. A foldable section (`FoldingSection`) springs its card's height open and shut (`motion.fold`) while its rows fade and settle in from 8 pt above, and its chevron turns from down to right; the cards below glide with it. On: filled (ink, or the aisle's `strong` shade for the aisle picker: `Pill hue=…`) with light text (700). - **Filled native header buttons** (`done` / `prominent` toolbar variants: Done, Save, the Recipes +): iOS draws their label white, so tint them with `prominentButtonTint` (`primary`, or `primarySoft` in dark mode, where `primary` is too light for white).
- **Nav row buttons** (inside native headers where the boards show them): 44 pt round `surface` buttons with `ink` glyphs; the primary action is `primary`.
